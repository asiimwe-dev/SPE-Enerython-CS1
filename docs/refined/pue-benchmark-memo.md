# PUE Benchmark Memo

**SPE AMTS Energython 2026 — Data Center Architecture & Load Profiling (CS #1)**

## 1. Executive Summary

Operating data centers in tropical Africa (ambient 35–42°C, 70%+ humidity) fundamentally changes PUE targets. Traditional air-cooled facilities achieve only 1.45–1.60 PUE in these conditions. This memo establishes a **target PUE of 1.25** justified by a hybrid cooling architecture combining air, direct-to-chip liquid, and single-phase immersion.

## 2. PUE Definition

$$\text{PUE} = \frac{E_{\text{Total}}}{E_{\text{IT}}} = \frac{E_{\text{IT}} + E_{\text{Cooling}} + E_{\text{Losses}} + E_{\text{Auxiliary}}}{E_{\text{IT}}}$$

$$\text{DCiE} = \frac{1}{\text{PUE}} \times 100\%$$

## 4. Tropical Climate Constraints

**Design conditions**: 35°C dry-bulb, 70% relative humidity, ambient range 25–42°C

- Traditional air-side economizers are **ineffective** for most of the year (wet-bulb temperature rarely drops below 24°C)
- Chillers must work against high ambient wet-bulb, increasing $E_{\text{Cooling}}$ significantly
- Liquid cooling technologies are **less sensitive** to ambient air temperature — their efficiency depends on coolant flow rates and heat exchanger design, not weather

## 5. Hybrid Cooling Architecture

Our target PUE of 1.25 is achieved by mixing cooling technologies across the three rack density zones:

| Zone | Density | Cooling Mix Weight | PUE Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zone A (250 racks, 20 kW) | Low | 40% air-cooled | Higher PUE per rack, but low absolute overhead |
| Zone B (80 racks, 50 kW) | Medium | 35% D2C liquid | Moderate PUE, efficient for 30–50 kW range |
| Zone C (30 racks, 100 kW) | High | 25% immersion | Lowest PUE, highest absolute power savings |

### Weighted PUE Calculation

$$\text{PUE}_{\text{weighted}} = w_{\text{air}} \cdot \text{PUE}_{\text{air}} + w_{\text{D2C}} \cdot \text{PUE}_{\text{D2C}} + w_{\text{imm}} \cdot \text{PUE}_{\text{imm}}$$

With the configured mix (40% air, 35% D2C, 25% immersion), the weighted PUE falls in the **1.25–1.35** range, supporting our 1.25 design target.

## 7. Recommendation

Target **PUE 1.25** for the 15 MW gross facility, yielding **12.0 MW net IT capacity**. This is realistic for a tropical hybrid-cooled facility and provides a defensible efficiency claim for the techno-economic model. Using a temperate-climate value (e.g., 1.15) would underestimate fuel costs and overestimate profitability.
